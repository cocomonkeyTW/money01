《Rare Treasure》考卷訂正與語法複習

📝 核心單字與片語

  • fossil collecting (n.) 收集化石
  • earn money for the family (v. phr.) 賺錢貼補家用
  • prehistoric creatures (n.) 史前生物
  • be fascinated by... (adj. phr.) 對……著迷
  • rockslides / landslides (n.) 岩崩 / 塌方、土石流
  • groundbreaking discoveries (n.) 突破性、重大的發現

💡 關鍵句型訂正（避開常見錯誤）

  • 1. 描寫原因與目的（補強第 1 題）
    Their father took them to the cliffs to earn money for the family.
    💡 注意：是「他們的」爸爸，要用 Their 而不是 There。
  • 2. 複數受格代名詞的運用（補強第 2 題）
    Mary found rare fossils and sold them to the museum.
    💡 注意：化石 fossils 是複數，後面一定要用 them，不能用 it。
  • 3. 動詞過去式（補強第 7 題）
    She found many rare and complete fossils.
    💡 注意：故事發生在過去，動詞 find 必須改成過去式 found。

✍️ 迷你語法驗收測驗

1. ______ father was a carpenter who taught them how to find fossils.
  • A) There
  • B) Their
  • C) They
2. Mary found two pterodactyl fossils in 1828 and sold ______ to scientists.
  • A) it
  • B) them
  • C) its
3. Many years ago, Mary Anning ______ (find) the first complete Plesiosaur.
  • A) find
  • B) finds
  • C) found
4. Collecting fossils was dangerous because the cliffs were unstable and ______ could happen.
  • A) fossils
  • B) landslides
  • C) museums
5. Mary was deeply ______ by prehistoric creatures and dinosaurs.
  • A) fascinated
  • B) fascinating
  • C) fascinate
